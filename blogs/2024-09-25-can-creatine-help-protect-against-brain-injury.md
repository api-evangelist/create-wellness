While most people know creatine primarily as a supplement for boosting muscle strength and endurance, its neuroprotective properties are garnering attention from scientists and healthcare professionals alike. As we delve into creatine's remarkable role in brain injury recovery, we will explore the mechanisms by which it supports cellular energy metabolism, reduces oxidative stress, and fosters brain resilience.
