In fitness and nutrition, creatine has long been hailed as a powerhouse supplement for enhancing athletic performance, building muscle, and improving recovery. Yet, despite its popularity, a common question lingers––does creatine affect women differently than men? As more women embrace strength training and high-intensity workouts, understanding how this supplement impacts both genders is crucial for optimizing performance and achieving fitness goals.
